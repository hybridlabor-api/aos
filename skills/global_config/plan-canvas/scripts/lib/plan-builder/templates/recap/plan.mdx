## What changed

Failed exports used to disappear: the job died, the customer saw a spinner and support had to re-run it by hand. Exports now retry up to three times with growing delays, and the export list shows each job as Queued, Running, Retrying or Failed.

- Retry delays are 1, 5 and 25 minutes.
- A job that exhausts its retries stays visible with the last error message.
- Customers can re-run a failed export with one click.
