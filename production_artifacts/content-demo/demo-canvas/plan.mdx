Fictional Pulse show-control app, component Cue editor UI. The design canvas (canvas.mdx) shows BEFORE and AFTER with numbered markers on AFTER. All names and numbers are invented.

</Callout>

## Summary {#summary}

- **What:** cues in the Cue editor UI are reordered by dragging a handle. The up/down buttons are gone.
- **Why:** moving a cue 12 places took 12 clicks, and a mis-click mid-show was easy.
- **Scope:** cue list interaction only. Cue data, timing and the engine are untouched.

## What changed {#changes}

The numbers match the markers on the AFTER artboard.

1. **Drag handles.** Every row has a grip on the left; rows no longer carry up/down buttons.
2. **Insertion line.** A blue line shows where the cue will land while dragging.
3. **Numbers stay stable.** Cue numbers are kept; only the order changes.
4. **Undo toast.** "Cue moved - Undo" appears for 6 seconds after a drop.
5. **Keyboard fallback.** Alt + Up/Down moves the selected cue, so the list works without a mouse.
